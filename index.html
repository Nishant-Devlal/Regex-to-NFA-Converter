How It Works

The converter follows the steps of Thompson's Construction.

1

Enter Regular Expression

Enter a regular expression using the supported operators.

2

Process Expression

The expression is tokenized and converted into postfix notation.

3

Apply Thompson's Construction

The postfix expression is converted into an equivalent ε-NFA.

4

View the NFA

The generated states and transitions are displayed as an NFA.
